Process systems engineering · Auburn University

Modeling processes.
Evaluating sustainable technologies.

I am Erfan Hosseini, a Ph.D. candidate in Chemical Engineering at Auburn University. I develop computational models to understand biochemical conversion and evaluate the economic and environmental performance of waste-to-energy technologies.

My research connects dynamic process modeling and kinetic parameter estimation with process simulation, techno-economic analysis, and life-cycle assessment. I study how feedstocks, operating conditions, and design choices shape the performance of bioenergy systems.

Featured research

When does converting mill sludge into energy make sense? My TEA/LCA study compares anaerobic digestion, alkaline pretreatment, and food-waste co-digestion under combined heat and power and biomethane pathways. The results reveal a tradeoff between lower climate impacts and higher costs relative to landfilling under the modeled assumptions.
