Research Experience

  • Graduate Researcher, Process Systems EngineeringAug 2022 – Present
    Auburn University

    • Evaluated anaerobic digestion and biogas upgrading strategies for pulp and paper mill sludge valorization under combined heat and power and biomethane production pathways.
    • Built reusable Python workflows for simulation, parameter analysis, visualization, reporting, and large-scale scenario comparison to support collaborative process evaluation and decision-making.
    • Developed modular Python code to run large sets of process simulations, compare operating scenarios, and generate datasets for future optimization and machine learning surrogate models.
    • Extended current Ph.D. research toward microstructure-informed process modeling by representing pulp and paper mill sludge as a heterogeneous fibrous structure and quantifying cellulose accessibility for hydrolysis modeling.
